# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

## Who we are and what this covers

Sapp Capital Advisors LLC (SCA, we, us, or our) provides commercial real estate underwriting and advisory services. This policy explains how we handle personal information through sapp.capital, www.sapp.capital, underwriting.sapp.capital, and SCA marketing pages and forms that link to this policy, including lead forms on Facebook and Instagram.

Contact us about privacy at [help@sapp.capital](mailto:help@sapp.capital) or [+1 336-568-6359](tel:+13365686359). Our mailing address is Sapp Capital Advisors LLC, 447 Broadway, 2nd Floor Suite #2133, New York, NY 10013, United States.

## Information we collect

You may give us your name, email address, company, phone number, communication preferences, scheduling details, and information about your firm's goals, investment activity, or proposed work. We also keep records of inquiries, applications, correspondence, email subscriptions, consent notices, and opt-out requests. If you use restricted client downloads, we process account and access information needed to authenticate you and confirm eligibility.

Our hosting and security providers process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps, and error logs. Where enabled and allowed by your choices, analytics and advertising tools process page views, clicks, campaign identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and cookie or device identifiers. Marketing emails may use tracking pixels and tracked links to measure opens and clicks; email software can limit the accuracy of these measurements.

We may receive information from scheduling providers, marketing service providers, and Meta when you submit a lead form or interact with our campaigns. Please do not enter payment card details, government identification numbers, or confidential deal files into public marketing forms. Confidential client work is handled under the applicable engagement and confidentiality arrangements.

## How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, assess whether we can help, arrange and follow up on calls, provide requested materials and services, manage client access, maintain business records, protect our sites, and comply with legal obligations. We also use campaign and engagement information to understand which marketing is useful and improve our sites and communications.

When you opt in, including by submitting a form that clearly explains the email subscription, we send SCA updates and marketing emails. You can unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email or contact help@sapp.capital. We may still send messages needed to answer your request, manage an appointment, or provide an agreed service. An email signup or call booking does not authorize automated marketing texts or prerecorded calls; any such program requires its own notice and permissions.

## Text messages and SMS consent

Sapp Capital Advisors LLC offers separate, optional text-message choices for appointment and service communications and for marketing. We send appointment confirmations, reminders, inquiry follow-up, and requested service updates only when you select the service-text option. We send promotional service updates, educational resources, and offers only when you select the marketing-text option. Either, both, or neither may be selected. Providing a phone number, subscribing to emails, booking a call, or submitting an application by itself does not give SMS consent. SMS marketing consent is not required to purchase services or submit a form.

We retain each submitted text preference, the phone number, disclosure wording and version, source form address, submission reference, and server-recorded time to document your choices. An unchecked option does not grant permission for that category. We also keep opt-out and suppression records. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from texts and HELP for assistance, or contact help@sapp.capital. Further details appear in our [Terms of Use](https://www.sapp.capital/terms).

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or use SMS opt-in records or consent for advertising audiences. Those records are excluded from the other marketing-sharing descriptions in this policy. We disclose mobile information and SMS consent records only to providers and support subcontractors that need them to operate our messaging service for us, or where disclosure is legally required. We do not provide that information to another party for its own marketing.

## Advertising and remarketing

We advertise SCA on Meta platforms and use remarketing to reach people who have interacted with our business. We do not operate third-party advertising space on these websites. Depending on the campaign and tools enabled, Meta may receive browser events through tools such as Meta Pixel or Conversions API, or contact identifiers used for audience matching. These help measure advertising and show relevant SCA ads on other services. Mobile information and SMS opt-in records and consent are excluded from audience matching and remarketing disclosures. We do not send confidential deal materials or application free-text answers to advertising tools.

We do not sell personal information for money. Disclosing identifiers or activity for targeted advertising may nevertheless be treated as a sale or sharing under applicable privacy law. Use Cookie Settings on our sites to decline optional website tracking. To request exclusion from contact-list advertising audiences, email help@sapp.capital with the email address used to contact us. Meta's own settings also let you manage advertising preferences. Website cookie choices do not control tracking on Meta, external booking pages, or other providers' sites.

## Cookies and your choices

Our [Cookie Policy](https://www.sapp.capital/cookies) describes necessary storage, optional analytics, and advertising technologies. Use the Cookie Settings control to accept or reject optional categories. On the two SCA sites covered by those controls, optional analytics and advertising tracking are off until you choose to allow them. We treat a recognized Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal as a request to decline those optional categories. An email subscription is separate from cookie permission.

## Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including ongoing inquiries, client services, business records, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. We consider the nature of the information, our relationship with you, and applicable recordkeeping requirements. We may keep a limited suppression record after an unsubscribe or deletion request to avoid contacting you again. Backup copies may remain until replaced through normal retention cycles.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Our business and providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.
