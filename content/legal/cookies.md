# Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

## Scope

Sapp Capital Advisors LLC uses cookies and similar browser storage on sapp.capital, www.sapp.capital, and underwriting.sapp.capital. This policy supplements our [Privacy Policy](https://www.sapp.capital/privacy). Questions: [help@sapp.capital](mailto:help@sapp.capital).

## Necessary storage

Necessary storage supports privacy choices, security, sign-in, and requested site features. Our sca_privacy_choices cookie records your optional tracking preferences for up to 180 days. On production SCA domains, it is shared across the main site and underwriting portal. Authentication providers may use additional cookies where client sign-in is enabled. Forms may use session storage to carry a request or campaign context between steps. These functions do not require optional analytics permission.

## Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures page views and interactions such as booking-link clicks and template downloads. Google may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish browsers and sessions. Their duration depends on the configured Google service and may extend up to two years. See [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Optional advertising and remarketing

SCA uses Meta for advertising and remarketing. Where website advertising tools are enabled, technologies such as Meta Pixel may use cookies such as _fbp or _fbc and process visit or campaign events to measure ads and build audiences. Those cookies commonly last up to 90 days, subject to Meta's settings and browser limits. Our website controls require advertising permission before optional website advertising tracking is allowed. A permission category does not mean every listed tool is installed on every page. See [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

We do not display third-party advertising space on these sites. Our remarketing promotes SCA on other platforms. Separately, campaign source parameters may be included in an inquiry you submit, and contact-list audiences may be managed through our marketing providers. A website cookie choice alone does not remove a contact from those audiences; contact help@sapp.capital to request exclusion.

## Your choices

Optional analytics and advertising are off until you choose to allow them. Use Cookie Settings to accept all, reject optional tracking, or choose categories separately. You can reopen the control on either site and change your choice. We treat recognized Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals as a request to decline optional tracking, even if a previously saved choice allowed it.

Changing a choice reloads the page so optional scripts can stop. We also attempt to remove known first-party analytics and advertising cookies when permission is withdrawn. We cannot remove data already received by another company, cookies outside our domains, or every browser-managed identifier. Contact us for requests concerning previously collected information.

Preferences apply in the browser where they are saved. They may need to be set again after clearing storage, in another browser, or on another device. If a browser blocks preference storage, optional tracking stays off. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect sign-in or other necessary features.

## Other services and email

External services, including Meta, Cal.com, and embedded or linked third-party content, may process information under their own settings and policies when you use them. Our cookie controls do not operate those external services. Email open and click measurements are separate from website cookies; unsubscribe from marketing emails or contact help@sapp.capital about email preferences.

## Updates

We will update this policy when tools or practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
